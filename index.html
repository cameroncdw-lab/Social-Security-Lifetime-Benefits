Federal tax on SS benefits

The IRS taxes SS benefits based on your provisional income — your other income plus 50% of your SS benefit. Up to 85% of your benefit can be taxable.

85%

These thresholds haven't been inflation-adjusted since 1994. Source: IRS Pub. 915; SmartAsset 2026.

Provisional income = other income + tax-exempt interest + 50% of annual SS benefit. Taxable portion × marginal rate = estimated annual SS tax. This is an estimate — consult a tax advisor for your actual liability.
