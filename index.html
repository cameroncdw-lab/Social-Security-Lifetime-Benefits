Still working while collecting?

If you claim before FRA and keep earning, SSA withholds $1 for every $2 you earn above $22,320/yr (2026). In your FRA calendar year the limit rises to $59,520 ($1 per $3). These withheld months are NOT lost — at FRA your monthly benefit is permanently raised to credit them back.

Federal tax on SS benefits

The IRS taxes SS based on your provisional income (other income + 50% of SS). Up to 85% of your benefit may be taxable.

Lifetime comparison

Spend checks: You claim early and spend each SS check as it arrives. Shows raw cumulative dollars received by life expectancy.

Invest early checks: You claim early and invest every check at the return rate above. Early claimers get more years of compounding — this answers "would investing beat delaying?"

Delay opportunity cost: You delay claiming but invest what you would have received from an earlier age. Asks "what if you bridged the gap by drawing from savings while waiting for a bigger SS check?"
